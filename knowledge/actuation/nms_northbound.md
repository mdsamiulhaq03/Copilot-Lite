# `nms_northbound` (placeholder)

## What it would target

The operator's existing network management system, instead of touching devices directly.

## Why it matters more than it looks

This is the shape most brownfield customers will actually want. Their NMS owns change
control, and an optimisation platform that writes around it is a procurement problem rather
than a product. Driving the NMS keeps their audit trail intact.

## Why it stays a placeholder

The concrete API is per customer, so there is nothing generic to build until a specific
integration is funded. What is reserved here is the seam and the key.

## Claims

No vendor interoperability is claimed.
